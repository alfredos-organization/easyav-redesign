Services

One partner from first walk-through to the service call five years later.

  1. Consulting

    Needs assessment, goals and objectives, audits of existing systems, and a technology plan that fits the room and the budget.

  2. Design / Build

    Concepts, design documents, 3D modeling and equipment specifications built on industry best practice.

  3. Installation

    Project management, systems engineering, implementation, testing and construction documents.

  4. Programming

    Control system design and touch-panel interfaces on AMX, Crestron, Extron and SP Control — tested until it's obvious.

  5. Training

    Full system walk-throughs, interactive whiteboard training, quick-start guides and skill assessments for your staff.

  6. Service & Maintenance

    Service contracts, general maintenance and repair, bench service, and emergency response when the meeting can't wait.

  • 05Johns Hopkins HospitalVideo conferencing & imaging integration

    Response. We installed a Polycom conferencing system and integrated the new imaging system into the hospital's existing network.

  • 06Smithsonian's National ZooInteractive whiteboard — Think Tank exhibit

    Need. An interactive classroom for summer campers inside the orangutan-memory research exhibit, hidden from general visitors.

  • 08Barnard Elementary SchoolAuditorium sound & video, 23 smart classrooms
